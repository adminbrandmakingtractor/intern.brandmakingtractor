Who We Are

An Internship Built Around Real Work

intern.brandmakingtractor.com is the internship program of Brand Making Tractor, an agency-led ecosystem spanning digital marketing, SEO, creative and web development. It exists for one reason: to give beginners a practical way into the industry — not more theory, but real client work done under real supervision.

The program is built for students, fresh graduates and aspiring digital professionals who learn faster by doing. Instead of assignments that get graded and forgotten, every intern's work is part of an actual live client project — which is why the structure stays the same across every track: one week of structured training, then straight onto real work for the rest of the chosen duration.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is intern.brandmakingtractor.com?

intern.brandmakingtractor.com is the internship program of Brand Making Tractor. It combines one week of structured training with a real, live client project, across seven specialized tracks.

Is intern.brandmakingtractor.com a training institute or an internship program?

It is an internship program, not a training institute. After one week of structured training, interns work directly on a live client project under supervision — there are no mock projects or classroom-only coursework.

Do interns work on live client projects?

Yes. Every internship track moves from one week of training straight into a real, live client project for the remainder of the chosen duration.

How does the internship program work?

Interns apply for a track and choose a duration (4, 6 or 8 weeks), complete one week of structured training, then work on a live client project under supervision, building experience they can carry into their careers.

What internship tracks are available?

Seven tracks are currently open: Data Analyst, Performance Marketing, Website Development (Custom Coded), Graphic Designing, Content Creation, WordPress Website Development, and Video Editing.

Who can apply for an internship?

The program is designed for students, fresh graduates, beginners and aspiring digital professionals who want practical, hands-on exposure rather than only theoretical learning.

What skills can I learn during the internship?

Skills depend on the track chosen — for example, data cleaning and dashboards in Data Analyst, Google/Meta Ads in Performance Marketing, custom or WordPress website development, brand design, content writing, or video editing. Each track page lists the specific skills covered.

Is an internship certificate provided?

Yes. Every intern who successfully completes their program receives a Certificate of Completion, verifiable with a Credential ID and QR code. Exceptional performers may also receive a Recommendation Letter, based on performance.

How can I apply?

Choose a track from the Internships page and submit an application, selecting your preferred duration. You can also apply directly from this page using the "Apply Now" button.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is intern.brandmakingtractor.com?

intern.brandmakingtractor.com is an internship program by Brand Making Tractor that combines structured training with practical and live client project experience.

How long is the training?

The initial structured training period is one week, guided by the relevant manager/team.

What happens after the one-week training?

After the initial training, interns move into direct practical work and live client project experience under team supervision.

What internship durations are available?

Internships are available in 4, 6 and 8-week durations.

What internship tracks are available?

The current internship tracks include Data Analyst, Performance Marketing, Website Development, Graphic Designing, Content Creation, WordPress Development and Video Editing.

Who can apply?

The program is designed for students, beginners, fresh graduates and aspiring professionals interested in building practical skills.

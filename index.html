FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the duration of the internship?

Internships are available in 4, 6 and 8-week durations. Every duration starts with one week of structured guided training, followed by practical work and live client project experience for the rest of the program.

Is training provided before practical work?

Yes. The first week is structured, guided training in your chosen track, so you understand the fundamentals and the workflow before moving into practical assignments.

Will I work on live client projects?

After the training week, interns participate in practical assignments and live client project workflows under supervision. The exact tasks depend on your internship track and current project requirements.

Who supervises the internship?

Your internship is supervised by the program manager and the responsible team for your internship track, who guide your work and share feedback.

Will I receive an internship certificate?

Yes, on successfully meeting the program requirements. After completing the internship, submit a request on the Request Certificate page; once reviewed and approved, your certificate is issued with a unique certificate ID and a verification QR code.

How can I verify my certificate?

Open the Verify Certificate page and enter the unique certificate ID printed on the certificate, or scan the QR code on it. The page confirms whether the certificate is genuine and currently valid.

What happens if I do not complete the internship requirements?

Certificates are issued only after the program requirements are met. If the requirements are not completed, the certificate request may not be approved. If you are facing difficulties, contact the team at internship@brandmakingtractor.com.

How do I apply?

Go to Explore Internships, choose your preferred track and click Apply. Fill in the application form, select a 4, 6 or 8-week duration and follow the payment instructions shown. You will receive a confirmation with your application number.
